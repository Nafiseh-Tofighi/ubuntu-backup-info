Ubuntu Backup privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Ubuntu Backup is a personal tool operated by Nafiseh Tofighi for her own computer and Google Drive account.

Data and purpose

The tool accesses files and folders within Desktop and Downloads only, including names, sizes, modification times and checksums, to maintain backups. Files over 1,000,000,000 bytes are excluded from automatic upload and recorded in a local report. Uploads occur after the owner confirms the upload prompt.

Google Drive access

The tool requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage backup files associated with the application. File content and metadata are transferred to the owner’s Google Drive through rclone. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner’s restricted rclone configuration. They are not published on this website.

Storage and sharing

Backups are ordinary files in Google Drive, without additional application-level encryption. Transfers use HTTPS. Google processes the stored data under its own terms and privacy policy. The tool does not sell data, use it for advertising or AI training, or send it to other services. This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal website access logs under its privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Current backups remain until the owner deletes them. Previous versions of replaced files are kept in the history folder. Deleting a local file does not automatically delete its Drive copy. The owner can stop the scheduled backup, revoke the application’s Google account access, remove the local configuration and reports, and delete the Nafiseh_Backup folder in Drive. Revoking access stops future access but does not itself delete existing backups.

Contact

Contact the operator through Nafiseh Tofighi’s GitHub profile or the repository issue tracker. Do not post private files or credentials in public issues.
